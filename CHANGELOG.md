# Changelog

## v1.1.0

- Colours, on devices with a colour screen (like the Kobo Libra Colour). The palette is tuned for
  colour e-ink: text stays dark and backgrounds stay light, so everything remains readable.
  Black-and-white devices stay strictly black on white.
- The up and down arrow keys are back in the key bar, for command history and moving around in
  `vi`, `less` and friends.

## v1.0.0

- First release: a real terminal inside Kobo's own interface, opened from NickelMenu, with Kobo's
  on-screen keyboard, extra keys, scrollback, Wi-Fi kept on while open, and a clean uninstall.
